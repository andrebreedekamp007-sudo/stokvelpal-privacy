Account Deletion

Delete your StokvelPal account

You have the right to request deletion of your StokvelPal account and your personal data at any time. This page explains how to request deletion and what happens to your data afterwards.

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your group members will lose access to records associated with your account.

How to request account deletion

  • Send an email to andre.breedekamp007@gmail.com with the subject line: StokvelPal Account Deletion Request

  • Include the mobile phone number linked to your StokvelPal account so we can identify your account.

  • We will confirm receipt of your request within 5 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

  • You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted.

Email us to delete your account

What gets deleted and what gets kept

Data type What happens Reason
Your phone number Deleted Removed from our authentication system
Your name and profile Deleted Personal profile data is fully removed
Your disclaimer acceptance record Deleted Legal acceptance record removed with account
Contribution records in group ledgers Anonymised Other group members have a legitimate interest in the financial history of their stokvel. Records are kept but your name and phone number are replaced with "Deleted Member".
Proof of payment photos you uploaded Retained for 90 days then deleted Other members may need to reference recent payment proofs. Deleted permanently after 90 days.
Audit log entries Anonymised Audit logs are immutable by design to protect group financial integrity. Your identity within them is anonymised.

If you are a group treasurer

If you are the treasurer of one or more stokvel groups, deleting your account will affect those groups. Before requesting deletion, we strongly recommend:

  • Transferring the treasurer role to another member
  • Downloading or sharing final statements with your group members
  • Notifying your group that you are closing your account

If you delete your account without transferring the treasurer role, the group will be archived and members will lose access to the group dashboard.
